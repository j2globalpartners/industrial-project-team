# Documentary sources — grid connection, site, insurance, supply, sectors

Cross-cutting reference. Lists, by role, the authoritative documents that support the role sheet, and the facts that can be drawn from them. This is the family in which the rules change fastest: several of the texts cited date from 2026.

- In France, RTE issues its technical and financial proposal within three months; the first instalment is paid on acceptance of the proposal (délibération n° 2026-32) [V]. **The procedure was revised twice in 2026.** For a distribution operator applying for connection, the annex to délibération n° 2026-173 allows three months to accept, extendable once by three months at most, on a written request received ten working days before the deadline [V]; the rule applicable to a consumer or a generator has not been re-read [NC].
- In France, Enedis: **the connection offer (generation), like the connection proposal (consumption), is valid for three months from the date it is sent; failing a reply it lapses, with no extension possible, and the project leaves the queue**; Enedis sends a reminder ten working days before the deadline [V, procedures Enedis-PRO-RES_67E and Enedis-PRO-RAC_14E as published on 13 November 2026]. The sheet previously said "typically 3 to 6 months"; it has been corrected accordingly.
- In France, réfaction (the share of the connection cost borne by the network tariff): the CRE note gives distinct rates by category — 40% for consumption, 60% on a sliding scale for small generation facilities — in a table that the reading rendered poorly [V partial]. **The CRE proposes to change the definition of these rates** (consultation n° 2026-09 du 13 mai 2026) [V]. Do not quote any rate without re-reading the text in force.
- In France, alternative connection offers (with curtailment): provided for by the code de l'énergie [French energy code] on the generation side; their extension to all users is under discussion [S].
- In France, discharges: the authorisation to discharge to the public sewer network falls under the code de la santé publique [French public health code]; silence for more than four months amounts to refusal [S].
- Outside France: financial guarantees on applying for connection in Spain (per kW) and in Poland (2026 reform) [S]. Nothing reliable found on Belgium.

- In France, third-party appeals against an environmental permit: **two months** for decisions taken on or after 1 September 2024 (four previously) [V]; notification of the appeal to the author and to the beneficiary within fifteen clear days, failing which it is inadmissible [V]. Starting point of the time limit [S]. Consistent with the sheet.
- In France, building permit: two months from the first day of a continuous two-month display on the site [S].
- In France, a derogating litigation regime has existed for certain projects since 1 July 2026 [S, content not read].
- In France, Commission nationale du débat public (CNDP): the 2024 referral sheet gives thresholds in investment amount by category of operation, with a mandatory referral threshold and a publication threshold half as high [V partial, table poorly rendered]; the scope was rediscussed in 2026: **check the article in force** [NC].
- In France, SAFER: two months to pre-empt from a complete notification by the notary, silence amounting to a waiver [S].
- In France, bail emphytéotique [long lease]: from more than 18 years to 99 years, a right in rem that can be mortgaged. Bail à construction [building lease]: from 18 to 99 years. A promise to sell lasting more than 18 months granted by a natural person must be executed as a notarial deed [S].
- IFC standards: engagement combines disclosure of information, consultation and a grievance mechanism, the latter complementing engagement without replacing it [E].

- Defects exclusion clauses of the London Engineering Group: LEG 1 is the outright exclusion; LEG 2 excludes the cost that would have been incurred to rectify the defect before the damage; LEG 3 excludes only the cost of improvement [S]. The DE1 to DE5 series is its British equivalent; the clause-for-clause equivalences are debated.
- Advance loss of profits: the IMIA publishes a document dedicated to the extension of the period of cover and an account of the cover [V]; the period of insurance is defined in its 2008 publication and **must be extended if completion slips** [E, clause partially read].
- In France: the list of structures exempt from compulsory ten-year (décennale) liability insurance is strictly construed; a structure not listed remains subject to it, even if ancillary. The exclusion in the code civil [French civil code] is aimed at items of equipment with an exclusively professional function, not at the structures themselves [S, reported case law].
- FIDIC 2017: insurance is in clause 19, with professional indemnity insurance that may cover fitness for purpose if the contract so stipulates [S].
