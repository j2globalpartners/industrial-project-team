- A PDRI score of 200 or less is associated with a markedly better probability of success [E, original 1996 guide; to be checked for v5.0]. For small infrastructure projects, the CII reference point is 300: do not generalise the 200 threshold.
- The FEL Index is IPA's quantitative measure of the quality of project definition, which can be calculated at any stage from preliminary design to completion of the front-end deliverables, before authorisation; front-end loading aims to reduce the number of changes during execution [V].
- "About 35% of megaprojects succeed" (Merrow): second-hand quotation [NC].

## Estimator

| Source | URL | Access |
|---|---|---|
| AACE RP 18R-97, Cost Estimate Classification System — process industries | https://library.aacei.org/pgd01/pgd01.shtml | paid |
| Reproduction of the 18R-97 matrix by the Government of Canada | https://canadabuys.canada.ca/documents/pub/att/2021/10/28/2305b22cf3ce2931a9ca532a8274cba4/appendix_e_-_annex_7.2_-_classification_system_cost_estimate_matrix.pdf | free |

| AACE RP 40R-08, 42R-08, 44R-08 (contingency) | https://library.aacei.org/pgd02/pgd02.shtml | paid, free tables of contents |
| ASTM E2516-11 (reapproved 2024) | https://store.astm.org/e2516-11r24.html | paid |
| GAO-20-195G, web version | https://gaoinnovations.gov/cost-guide/introduction.html | free |

- AACE 18R-97 gives, for each class, **a range of ranges** and not a single value: Class 5, low side −20 to −50%, high side +30 to +100%; Class 1, low side −3 to −10%, high side +3 to +15% [E]. Classes 2 to 4: values not re-read [NC]. The single values in the sheet (−30/+50%, −20/+30%…) are points inside these ranges, not the standard.
- Maturity of definition is the only characteristic that determines the class; the actual range is determined by risk analysis [E].
- Lang factors: 3.10 (solids), 3.63 (solids-fluids), 4.74 (fluids); 1947 article [S].
- GAO: a 12-step process; the sensitivity analysis and then the risk and uncertainty analysis come before the documentation of the estimate [E].
- AACE 42R-08 (parametric) is the recommended method when systemic risks dominate, that is, in the early phases [E].
- AACE definition of contingency (10S-90): an amount added to an estimate for items whose state, occurrence or effect is uncertain and which experience shows will, in aggregate, result in additional costs. **It excludes major scope changes, extraordinary events, management reserve, escalation and currency effects, and it is expected to be spent** [V].

## Cost controller

| Source | URL | Access |
|---|---|---|
| NASA Earned Value Management Implementation Handbook (2018, rev. 2021) | https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/nasa-evm-implementation-handbook.pdf | free |
| GAO-20-195G, chapter "Project Future Performance" | https://gaoinnovations.gov/cost-guide/project-future-performance.html | free |
| NDIA IPMD, EVMS Intent Guide (EIA-748), version D (2018) | https://www.humphreys-assoc.com/evms/evms-documents/ndia/ndia_ipmd_intent_guide_ver_d_aug282018.pdf | free |

- Forecast-at-completion formulas: EAC = BAC / CPI; EAC = AC + (BAC − EV); EAC = AC + (BAC − EV) / (CPI × SPI). TCPI = (BAC − EV) / (BAC − AC) [S].
- According to the GAO, the EAC at cumulative CPI is rather a "best case"; CPI × SPI generally gives the highest EAC [E].
- EIA-748: 32 criteria in five families — organisation; planning and budgeting; accounting; analysis and reporting; revisions and data maintenance [E].
- Management reserve (AACE 10S-90): held outside the performance measurement baseline, with no scope, not tied to individual risks and not time-phased [V].

## Planner

| Source | URL | Access |
|---|---|---|
| DCMA-EA PAM 200.1, Program Analysis Pamphlet (2012) — contains the 14 points | https://www.dcma.mil/Portals/31/Documents/Policy/DCMA-PAM-200-1.pdf | free |

- DCMA thresholds: missing logic ≤ 5% of remaining tasks; no leads; lags ≤ 5% of relationships; finish-to-start relationships ≥ 90%; hard constraints ≤ 5%; high float (> 44 working days) ≤ 5%; high duration ≤ 5% [S, the first confirmed by the GAO]. CPLI and BEI thresholds: contradictory sources (0.95 or 1.0) [NC].
- The GAO states that these thresholds are not compliance criteria but "a starting point toward an objective analysis" [E].
- GAO-16-89G: ten best practices, tied to four characteristics of a reliable schedule — comprehensive, well-constructed, credible, controlled [V].
- AACE 37R-06: Level 1 presents the project by its major components, Level 2 subdivides them, and **Level 3 is the first level at which a meaningful critical path network can be displayed and used to control the whole project** [V, free excerpt of the recommended practice].

- FIDIC 2017, clause 20: notice of claim as soon as practicable and no later than 28 days after the party became aware, or should have become aware, of the event — **an obligation that falls on both parties**; failure to comply may forfeit the entitlement to claim [V, Fenwick Elliott note].
- Fully detailed claim within 84 days of becoming aware of the event, or another period agreed with the Engineer; this period was 42 days in the 1999 edition [V, same note].
- The Engineer must give notice within 14 days of receipt if he considers the notice to be late [V, same note]. Clause 3.7: 42 days of consultation, then 42 days for the determination; challenge within 28 days, failing which acceptance is deemed [S].
- SCL Protocol: 2nd edition published in February 2017, superseding the 1st; free to download, **with a French version** [V]. Six delay analysis methods, none preferred after the event; contemporaneous analysis to be favoured [S].
- In France, CCAG Travaux 2021: delay penalties capped at 10% of the contract amount [S; daily rate not confirmed].

- EP4 applies to project finance from USD 10 million of capital cost; in force since 1 October 2020 [E, S].
- Categories A (significant, irreversible impacts) and B (limited, reversible): grievance mechanism and independent environmental and social consultant. EP4 removed the assessment exemption for designated countries, France among them [S].
- DSCR = cash flow available for debt service / debt service for the period [E]. The ranges by sector (of the order of 1.2 to 1.35× with contracted revenue, more with market exposure) come only from training materials [NC].
